The details

Comprehensive breakdown

We do not collect personal data. PeriTrace operates entirely without a backend server, user accounts, or analytics and crash-reporting services embedded in the app. The app does not collect or transmit personal information to us, such as names, email addresses, precise locations, device identifiers, or usage statistics. Pattern data and captured images are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us or any third party by the app.

The only exception is when you choose to email us directly through the support page's contact form. This opens your own mail app, allowing you to send a message from your email address to ours. The message is sent at your request and handled like any other email you send.

PeriTrace requests camera permission so it can power its core AR pattern-tracing feature — detecting flat surfaces, showing the live camera feed, and letting you trace pattern pieces in augmented reality.

  • The camera feed is processed entirely on your device using Apple's ARKit framework.
  • PeriTrace does not continuously record or transmit the camera feed.
  • During automatic contour detection, PeriTrace may capture a high-resolution still image of the pattern piece. This image is processed entirely on your device to assist with contour detection and may be saved as part of the corresponding pattern piece.
  • During manual image capture, you can capture up to 10 high-resolution images of different areas within a traced polygon. These images are processed and merged into a single image entirely on your device and may be saved as part of the corresponding pattern piece.
  • Captured images remain on your device. PeriTrace does not transmit these images to Twill & Thrills or any third-party service.
  • You can choose whether captured images are shown and included in exported files using the image visibility option in the canvas menu.
  • Geometry you actively capture while tracing, such as vertices and marks, is also stored locally as part of your pattern data.
  • You can revoke camera access at any time in
    iOS Settings → PeriTrace → Camera.
    Without camera access, AR tracing, automatic contour detection, and manual image capture won't work, but the rest of the app (pattern management and canvas — where pattern pieces are viewed and organized) will remain available.

All patterns, pieces, captured pattern images, and exported files you create in PeriTrace are stored locally on your iPhone.

Saved pattern data is stored locally in:
On My iPhone → PeriTrace → Saves
Pattern data uses the proprietary .peri format, while captured images associated with pattern pieces are stored as separate image files within the same Saves folder. These files are not transmitted to us or any third party by the app.

Exported files are automatically saved to:
On My iPhone → PeriTrace → Exports
Existing files with identical names may be overwritten during export. These files are only shared elsewhere if you explicitly choose a destination in the iOS Share Sheet (e.g., saving to Files, AirDrop, or sending to another app).

If you use iCloud device backups, some PeriTrace app data may be included, depending on Apple's backup rules and your device settings. Apple handles these backups under its own privacy practices; PeriTrace does not operate its own backup servers.

PeriTrace does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The app does not sell, rent, or transmit your locally stored pattern data or captured images to third parties.

The app relies on Apple's built-in frameworks, such as ARKit, to provide its features. PeriTrace processes camera imagery for tracing and image capture on-device. Apple's handling of information through its operating system and services is governed by Apple's own privacy practices.

Because PeriTrace collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children either. The app is a general-purpose pattern-digitizing tool and is not directed at children.

  • Camera permission: grant or revoke anytime in iOS Settings.
  • Your patterns: live exclusively on your device. You can delete them at any time from within PeriTrace or manually via the iOS Files app. There are no cloud-based copies on our infrastructure to also delete.
  • Uninstalling the app: Uninstalling PeriTrace removes its locally stored data from your device, but separately exported or backed-up files may remain. A local numeric token tracking your used trial exports will be stored securely in the iOS keychain. Due to iOS behavior, this token may persist after the app is uninstalled and may be accessed if the app is reinstalled.

If PeriTrace ever changes how it works — for example, if a future version adds an optional cloud sync feature — this policy will be updated first, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change. We will not start collecting data silently.
